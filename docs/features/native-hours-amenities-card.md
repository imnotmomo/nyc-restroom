## Data audit and acceptance

- The bundled native city snapshot contains restroom hours for 971 of 1,064 locations; 93 lack hours. Its source defines these as restroom access hours, not general business hours. All city rows lack the community entry-permission field, which made the previous prominent Entry value say Not reported despite known hours below it.
- Read-only public Firestore inspection found 103 community listings with no direct hours field, and one optional access-details record for Peninsula. Peninsula already has the supplied 24/7 restroom hours, right-side step-free entrance, elevator, floor and zero-stairs report. No database write is needed.
- Existing directory hours must remain visible when the optional sidecar feed is loading or unavailable. Unknown community schedules retain distinct loading, failure and successful-unknown states. Never turn unknowns into 24/7 or calculate current opening from free text.
- Show only supported positive amenities. Elevator requires explicit restroom-route evidence. Accessible uses the existing unified reported-status resolver and does not certify stall features. Stair free requires an explicit complete-route report. There is no structured automatic-door fact or reviewed evidence to display.
- Source research has 18 attributed hour facts covering 12 city locations, some conflicting with directory schedules. Keep those attributed in Details rather than silently replacing the schedule. Preserve the original hours text, limitations and report/observation/source dates.
- Use two short rows without repeated source/date paragraphs. Of the 971 known city schedules, 201 have seven weekday lines. Only a strict complete seven-day schedule using full English weekday names and an exact colon-space or tab delimiter may group adjacent days with identical reported hours; never parse time ranges into live opening status. Duplicate, missing or nonstandard weekday formats retain their original text, and the complete original always stays in Details. Detailed entry permission, right-side entrance, elevator instruction and floor must remain reachable. Keep all existing sign-in, rating, save, passcode and account-deletion protections intact.
