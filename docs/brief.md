# Restroom Ready — Product Brief

Restroom Ready is a proposed NYC restroom discovery tool for people who need a restroom beyond the places they already know. The main job is helping someone choose before making the trip.

## The problem

A nearby place on a map does not explain whether someone can enter, reach the restroom inside, or use its facilities. The supplied interview records and public Reddit accounts describe repeat searches, entry restrictions, missing supplies, and internal-route details. These accounts guide a prototype; they do not establish current venue conditions or customer demand.

## First product slice

Build a phone-friendly listing comparison in Next.js. Each listing should separate:

- entry rules, including purchase, guest, key, or code requirements;
- the restroom floor and route inside, including stairs or assistance where reported;
- reported conditions such as supplies, usable stalls, and floor condition;
- the source and observation time for each fact, with submission time separate;
- missing information and conflicting reports.

Prototype records must be labeled as examples. Browse and comparison should not require an account. A short contribution form is proposed for later testing; contribution rates are unknown.

## Acceptance for the first slice

A user can compare two example listings on a narrow phone screen and find entry rules, internal route, reported condition, and unknown fields without opening a long review. Observation time and submission time remain distinct. Updating one condition field does not change the date of the route report. A missing elevator report is displayed as unknown.

## Boundaries

The first slice does not certify accessibility, promise current availability or cleanliness, claim complete NYC coverage, or implement paid features. Real listings need real sources before publication. The selected logo is 03 — R tile. The style guide records implementation rules for the three retained page directions, B/E/F, for later comparison.
