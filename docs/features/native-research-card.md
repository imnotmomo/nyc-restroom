# Native research facts in the restroom card

Status: source, pure-model and linked-SDK checks complete; the final Search/List half-height check passed, October 24, 2026. Long-scroll UI automation remains a documented limitation. This revision has not been uploaded to TestFlight.

The card reads the selected place's administrator-curated `restroomResearchProfiles/{placeID}` document. Hours, Amenities, Condition and Passcodes show the available information directly, with expandable facts in the corresponding row or destination. Entry & route retains the exact entrance, floor and route instructions. Rate and Comment contains published comments alongside visitor contributions, and separately labeled external source scores. The card does not send research text to Analytics or the on-device visitor-summary model.

The new profile packet contains 1,146 places, 31,959 facts and nine narrative comments. All 1,064 city places and 82 community places have a profile. A missing profile preserves the existing place facts and unknowns. The packet retains all original meaningful research fields, including room-specific Parks fixture counts, feature grades and reported issues; the compact card expands these in their relevant section instead of displaying dozens of rows initially.

Known restroom hours remain visible during research loading or failure. Venue hours retain their venue qualification. Building accessibility, including the directory accessibility field, does not establish the complete restroom route. An explicit sourced closure warning remains visible beside its source/date even when an older directory listing says Operational. Inspection grades and issue counts retain the actual inspection day and room context; they do not become cleanliness stars. Publication, inspection and retrieval dates remain separate, and absent original dates stay unknown.

The [bounded hours audit](research-hours-audit-20261024.json) distinguishes 973 places with listed/restroom schedules, 58 with venue hours only and 136 without a schedule among the 1,167 places. No accepted schedule was lost in projection. The native successful-empty state says Hours unknown; loading and failures remain separate. Seasonality-only information, such as Year Round, stays in the expanded Hours details and cannot become a schedule. Qualified source wording such as Open by permit stays unchanged.

Historical online code claims retain their text, including leading zeros, under Passcodes. They are marked unverified and have no successful-use counts, ownership, Used or Code didn't work controls. Actual user code reports, exact-version feedback, visibility and ownership are unchanged. Published narrative comments display as Anonymous with their original source/date. Existing source comments are suppressed only when the same place's confirmed valid profile explicitly covers their exact IDs; loading or failure restores the legacy comments.

The profile listener uses exact place identity, selection-generation guards, server-confirmed snapshots and the existing memory-only Firestore cache. It removes its listener with the existing selected-place lifecycle. Invalid documents fail closed and cannot erase existing base facts or a previously valid same-place profile. This is a read-only namespace for the app; authentication, ratings and contribution mutations are unchanged.

All restroom openings use one medium-only sheet, including accessibility text sizes. Long cards and focused destinations scroll within that sheet. Account, filters and contribution-form presentation keep their existing behavior. Search and List reopenings are checked against the same half-height boundary; map selections use the same central place-sheet branch.

Independent source review approved the profile schema, state boundaries, direct sections and protected contribution behavior. The pure checks include every profile in the exact published packet, date-kind preservation, room-grade/issue summaries, closure warnings, zero fixture counts, leading-zero code claims, strict schema/field/scope/source references, unsafe links and the document-size bound. Actual simulator evidence and the final frozen source hashes will be recorded after the final half-height candidate passes.
